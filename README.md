# Iron Log

A personal gym tracker that installs on your phone: weekly plans, workout logging with a rest timer and plate maths, progress charts, and a Food tab that turns a goal (lean bulk, bulk, cut, recomp, maintain) into calories, protein, BMI and daily chicken and egg amounts.

**Open it:** https://frhandnial.github.io/iron-log/

## Install

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, then ⋮ menu → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, then Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

It works offline once installed.

## Your data

Everything you log stays on your phone (browser storage). Nothing is sent to this site. Use Settings → **Download backup** now and then; **Restore a backup file** loads it on a new phone.
